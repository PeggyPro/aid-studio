Emotion preview and local face detection assets

head.glb: Derived from ICT-FaceKit FaceXModel at commit
 da5f95a607f5e6b37755b38d3385d7f2853732e5
https://github.com/ICT-VGL/ICT-FaceKit
Copyright (c) 2020 USC Institute for Creative Technologies. MIT license:
see ICT-FaceKit-LICENSE.txt.
Changes: triangulation, metre units, a fictional blend of released PCA identity
modes, a slight neutral eyeBlink so the upper lids rest on the iris, neutral
plaster materials, iris/limbus/pupil vertex shading, omitted translucent eye
overlays and untextured eyelashes, 22 expression shapes, compacted primitive
vertex buffers, geometry-derived cavity shading and ray-traced nostril
occlusion. Rebuild with scripts/studio/build-emotion-head.py (Python + numpy).
Presentation: bald plaster bust (LibTV-style emotion schematic) lit by the UI
with a room environment, a shadow-casting key and a rim light. Colored skin + procedural hair art pass
is optional and offline-only via scripts/studio/emotion_portrait.py; it is not
shipped in head.glb. Asset regression: python scripts/studio/test-emotion-head.py.

vision/: unmodified WASM runtime from @mediapipe/tasks-vision 0.10.32.
The .wasm.gz transport variants are lossless gzip copies of those same binaries,
generated by scripts/prepare-studio-emotion-vision.mjs before dev/build/export.
face-detector.tflite: Google MediaPipe BlazeFace short-range float16 model, v1.
https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/face_detector/blaze_face_short_range/float16/1/blaze_face_short_range.tflite
https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe
Copyright Google LLC. Apache License 2.0: see MediaPipe-LICENSE.txt.
All assets are served locally; no third-party CDN is required at runtime.
